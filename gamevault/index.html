Guest information may be stored locally on your device. Cloud accounts and synced game data are hosted using Supabase. Security and deletion audit records may be retained where reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations; retained deletion audits do not contain your email address or provider credentials.
